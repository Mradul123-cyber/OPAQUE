Delete Your Zarq Messenger Account

⚠️ Warning: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your data will be permanently removed from our servers.

What Will Be Deleted?

When you delete your account, the following data will be permanently removed:

  • Your profile information (username, display name, profile picture)
  • All your messages and conversations
  • All media files you've sent or received
  • Your contacts and friendships
  • Call history and logs
  • All encryption keys associated with your account
  • Device information

How to Delete Your Account

To request account deletion:

  1. Send an email to opaquelabs.in@gmail.com
  2. Use the subject line: "Account Deletion Request"
  3. Include the following information in your email:
    • Your registered phone number
    • Your username (if you remember it)
    • Confirmation that you want to permanently delete your account
Send Account Deletion Request

Processing Timeline

We will process your account deletion request within 7 business days of receiving your email.

You will receive a confirmation email once your account has been permanently deleted.

Before You Delete

Consider these alternatives:

  • Clear conversations: You can delete individual conversations without deleting your account
  • Change privacy settings: Adjust who can see your profile and online status
  • Temporary break: You can simply uninstall the app and reinstall later
Important: Once your account is deleted, you will need to create a new account if you want to use Zarq Messenger again in the future. Your old username may not be available.
